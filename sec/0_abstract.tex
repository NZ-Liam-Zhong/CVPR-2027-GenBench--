\begin{abstract}
We propose GenBench-$\infty$, a diverse benchmark testing the Zero-Shot generality capabilities of Embodied Foundation Models on various types of embodiments. We curated a 1000 scene dataset derived from egocentric videos, alongside a Real2Sim agentic framework to obtain scenes from aforementioned videos. We make use of concentrated agents as well as targeted use of skills in order to minimize the cost and time of scene creations, while maintaining high fidelity. Evaluation was performed on multiple embodied foundation models, and an analysis on the cost efficiency of robot deployment is provided.


\end{abstract}
